🎯 Problem Statement & Objectives

Plant diseases present a catastrophic threat to crop yield and agricultural food security. Manual inspection by human experts is expensive, slow, and unscalable in rural communities.

Core Project Objectives:

  • Automate disease detection directly from digital leaf photographs.
  • Distinguish between healthy leaf foliage and 37 distinct disease states.
  • Provide actionable organic remedies, chemical options, and preventive guidelines.
  • Deliver an intuitive, responsive web application for farmers and researchers.
  • Achieve high predictive accuracy using Transfer Learning Deep Neural Networks.
