Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Crop Insurance Scheme

Provides comprehensive insurance cover against crop loss due to non-preventable natural risks like severe fungal disease outbreaks, droughts, and floods.

Apply for PMFBY Coverage
🌱

Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY)

Organic Farming Subsidy

Financial assistance of ₹50,000 per hectare provided for organic inputs, neem extracts, bio-fungicides, and PGS organic certification.

View PKVY Guidelines
